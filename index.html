How the flock moves

Inspired by Craig Reynolds’ Boids. Each bird makes small decisions about its neighbours:

  • Separation: leave room.
  • Alignment: follow their heading.
  • Cohesion: stay near the group.

Those local choices create flocking without a leader. Your touch acts as an obstacle; nearby birds steer around it.

This is a simplified artistic simulation, not a biological model or an exact reproduction.

Reduce Motion is enabled. The scene stays still; touches are still acknowledged.
